[SIZE=6][COLOR=#ff69b4][B]Why PinkyTeams?[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
PinkyTeams is a complete, configurable clan system designed for survival, PvP and network servers. It combines everyday clan management with wars, persistent seasons, rankings, economy, custom roles, alliances, PlaceholderAPI and five storage engines.

[TABLE]
[TR][TD][B]Clan management[/B][/TD][TD]Creation, invitations, public/private access, custom tags, homes and leadership tools[/TD][/TR]
[TR][TD][B]Competition[/B][/TD][TD]Clan wars, kill scoring, anti-farming, timed matches, seasons and historical rankings[/TD][/TR]
[TR][TD][B]Economy[/B][/TD][TD]Vault or internal economy, clan bank, atomic deposits/withdrawals and persistent audit history[/TD][/TR]
[TR][TD][B]Customization[/B][/TD][TD]Editable languages, pink GUI theme, materials, slots, sounds, permissions and messages[/TD][/TR]
[TR][TD][B]Storage[/B][/TD][TD]YAML, SQLite, H2, MySQL and MariaDB[/TD][/TR]
[/TABLE]

[SIZE=6][COLOR=#ff69b4][B]Main Features[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
[/CENTER]
[LIST]
[*][B]Modern 54-slot clan dashboard[/B] with a live clan overview, members, invitations, alliances, bank, wars, home, rankings, roles and upgrades.
[*][B]Fully configurable GUI:[/B] change the pink filler, materials, positions and navigation sounds from [ICODE]config.yml[/ICODE].
[*][B]Persistent clan wars[/B] with requests, accept/decline, surrender, timers, kill scores, rewards and repeated-kill anti-farming.
[*][B]Season system[/B] with automatic or manual closure, point rankings, optional point resets, reward commands and historical snapshots.
[*][B]Clan economy[/B] with Vault/internal support, configurable limits, verified rollbacks and a persistent bank transaction log.
[*][B]Custom clan roles[/B] with assignable permissions, member management and in-game GUI controls.
[*][B]Alliances and friendly fire[/B] with requests, relationship controls and independent ally FF settings.
[*][B]Clan homes[/B] with configurable teleport delay, cooldowns and bypass permissions.
[*][B]Rankings[/B] for points, KDA, money and members, available through commands, GUI and PlaceholderAPI.
[*][B]Nametag privacy[/B] through the internal scoreboard, TAB or UnlimitedNametags-style integration modes.
[*][B]English and Spanish[/B] included, with editable YAML language files and runtime language selection.
[*][B]Discord webhooks[/B] for clan creation and leaderboard changes.
[*][B]UUID identity tracking[/B] that safely migrates memberships, leadership, invitations and statistics after name changes.
[*][B]Automatic VanguardClans migration[/B] with legacy permission and placeholder compatibility during the transition.
[*][B]Public API and Bukkit events[/B] for developers integrating creation, disband, membership and alliance activity.
[*]Shared ranking cache and asynchronous storage queries for better server performance.
[/LIST]

[CENTER][SIZE=6][COLOR=#ff69b4][B]Installation[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]
[LIST=1]
[*]Download [ICODE]pinkyteams-1.8.0.jar[/ICODE].
[*]Place it inside your server's [ICODE]/plugins[/ICODE] directory.
[*]Start the server once to generate the configuration and language files.
[*]Choose your storage engine in [ICODE]config.yml[/ICODE]. SQLite is the default and needs no external database.
[*]Optionally install Vault plus an economy plugin, PlaceholderAPI and TAB.
[*]Grant [ICODE]pinkyteams.user[/ICODE] to players and [ICODE]pinkyteams.admin[/ICODE] to administrators.
[/LIST]

[CENTER][SIZE=6][COLOR=#ff69b4][B]Requirements & Hooks[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]
[LIST]
[*][B]Required:[/B] Java 17 or newer and a Spigot/Paper-compatible 1.20+ server.
[*][B]Optional:[/B] Vault, PlaceholderAPI and TAB.
[*][B]Storage:[/B] YAML, SQLite, H2, MySQL or MariaDB. Database drivers are bundled.
[/LIST]

[CENTER][SIZE=6][COLOR=#ff69b4][B]Upgrading from VanguardClans[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
Keep the old data folder and install PinkyTeams. On first startup, the plugin creates a backup-aware one-time migration without deleting the original directory. Legacy permissions and [ICODE]%vanguardclans_*%[/ICODE] placeholders can remain enabled temporarily while you update external configurations.
